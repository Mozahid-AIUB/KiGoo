KiGoo Mobile App - Final Product Model (MVP v1)

🌍 About KiGoo

KiGoo is a student-first mobility platform designed to make university transportation safer, smarter, and more affordable.

KiGoo is built as a multi-service platform, where different transportation services will be available under one app.

Initial Launch

Only KiGoo Campus will be active.
